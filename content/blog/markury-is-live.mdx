After months of building, testing, and refining, **Markury is officially live**.

Markury is a modern screen annotation and presentation tool designed for educators, presenters, developers, designers, and remote teams who need a fast and flexible way to draw, highlight, and explain ideas directly on their screen.

Many professionals rely on annotation tools daily during:

- Online teaching
- Product demos
- Design reviews
- Remote collaboration
- Technical walkthroughs

But over time we noticed something: most tools hadn’t evolved with modern workflows.

So we built Markury to change that.

## What Makes Markury Different

Markury focuses on flexibility, speed, and clarity during live presentations.

Key capabilities include:

- Advanced color selection for precise highlighting
- Laser pointer for live presentations
- Flexible toolbar (horizontal or vertical layout)
- Modern UI with dark/light modes and theme colors
- Dynamic keyboard shortcuts
- Precision drawing tools including shapes like ellipse
- Tool visibility controls to keep your workspace clean
- Continuous feature updates and improvements

The goal is simple: **make explaining things on screen effortless.**

## Built for Modern Workflows

Whether you're teaching a class, presenting to clients, or walking a team through a product demo, Markury helps you communicate visually without interrupting your flow.

We designed Markury to stay lightweight, customizable, and continuously improving.
