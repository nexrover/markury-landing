When it comes to screen annotation software, Epic Pen has been a popular choice for years. However, as workflows evolve, users are increasingly looking for a more modern, lightweight, and versatile **Epic Pen alternative**. 

If you are a teacher, presenter, designer, or professional who needs to draw on your screen smoothly without slowing down your computer, **Markury** is the ultimate solution.

## What is Markury?

Markury is a powerful desktop screen annotation tool designed for macOS and Windows. It allows you to draw, highlight, and annotate directly over any application, presentation, or website with a beautifully crisp and modern interface.

Unlike older software that feels clunky, Markury provides a **floating toolbar** that stays out of your way until you need it. 

## Why Choose Markury Over Epic Pen?

Here are the top reasons why users are switching to Markury as their go-to Epic Pen alternative:

### 1. Modern and Intuitive Interface
Markury features a sleek, minimalist design that seamlessly integrates with modern operating systems (like macOS Sonoma and Windows 11). The toolbar is customizable—choose between horizontal or vertical orientations—and looks great in both light and dark modes.

### 2. Built for Performance
Many annotation tools lag when you draw quickly. Markury uses hardware acceleration to ensure every stroke is buttery smooth. It's lightweight and won't consume your CPU or RAM during important remote meetings or live streams.

### 3. Laser Pointer for Presentations
Need to guide your audience's eyes without leaving permanent marks on the screen? Markury's **Laser Pointer** tool leaves a glowing trail that automatically fades away after a few seconds—a feature perfect for Zoom presentations and online classes.

### 4. Advanced Shape Tools and Geometric Precision
While freehand drawing is great, sometimes you need to draw perfect circles, rectangles, and straight arrows to highlight UI elements or data points. Markury makes geometric annotations simple and precise.

### 5. Native Mac Support (Apple Silicon)
Finding a solid screen annotation tool for Mac has traditionally been difficult. Markury is built to run natively on Apple Silicon (M1/M2/M3), offering incredible performance and battery life for Mac users.
