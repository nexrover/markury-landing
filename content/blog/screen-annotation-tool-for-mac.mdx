## The Problem with Traditional Mac Annotation Tools

For years, Mac users have had to settle for subpar drawing tools. Many cross-platform tools run via emulation (like Rosetta 2) which drains battery life and causes input lag when drawing. Others clutter your screen with massive toolbars that get in the way of your actual work.

## Why Markury is the Best Annotation Tool for Mac

Markury solves these problems by focusing on three core pillars: **Performance, Aesthetics, and Usability**.

### 1. Optimized for Apple Silicon (M1/M2/M3)
Markury runs natively on Apple Silicon. This means zero lag when freehand drawing, instant app startup times, and minimal battery consumption—even when running alongside demanding applications like Zoom, Figma, or Final Cut Pro.

### 2. A Toolbar That Feels Like macOS
The Markury floating toolbar respects Apple’s Human Interface Guidelines. It features beautiful translucent backgrounds, smooth animations, and automatic light/dark mode switching based on your Mac’s system settings.

Control Markury without ever moving your mouse. You can customize global keyboard shortcuts (like `⌘ + Shift + D`) to instantly toggle drawing mode on and off, switch colors, or activate the laser pointer.
